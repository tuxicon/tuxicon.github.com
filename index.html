About Me

01

I am an Assistant Professor in the Min H. Kao Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, where I lead the EPIC Lab.

My research lies at the intersection of human-computer interaction (HCI), ubiquitous computing, and embodied AI. At the EPIC Lab, our mission is to address pressing community-centered challenges and advance sustainable development in areas such as environmental sustainability, education, health, and manufacturing.

We pursue this mission by designing and building novel technical systems, ranging from battery-free sensing devices and environmental monitoring platforms to human-centered AI platforms. We also study how people envision, interact with, and shape these technologies through community-engaged research, including work with urban farming communities and AI education initiatives in rural K–12 schools and other underserved settings.

Before joining UT Knoxville in 2022, I received my Ph.D. in Human-Computer Interaction from Carnegie Mellon University. I have also worked with research teams at Microsoft Research, Oak Ridge National Laboratory, INRIA, and Xerox Research.

Our research has been recognized with an NSF CAREER Award, an ACM SIGMOBILE Research Highlight, and ACM UbiComp/ISWC Distinguished Paper Awards for NeuroCamTags (2025) and RadioGami (2026). I also received the UTK Chancellor's Innovation Award (2025).
